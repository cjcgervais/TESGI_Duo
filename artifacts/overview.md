# TESGI Advisory — Decision Clarity Without Representation

## What This Service Is

TESGI Advisory provides **paid, non-representational decision analysis** for land, property, and development-related decisions.

The purpose of this service is to help clients:
- understand material risks and constraints,
- identify information gaps and assumptions,
- and decide whether to **proceed, pause, or walk away**.

This service focuses on clarity and informed judgment — not transactions.

---

## What This Service Is Not

TESGI Advisory does **not** provide:
- real estate representation,
- brokerage or agency services,
- negotiation or transaction support,
- guarantees, predictions, or forecasts,
- approvals, permitting outcomes, or valuation assurances.

TESGI does not act on behalf of clients in transactions.

---

## How Decisions Are Evaluated

All advisory work is structured using a three-part decision kernel:

### TRUE — Information Integrity  
Are material facts reasonably available, verifiable, and clearly bounded?  
Are uncertainties explicitly identified?

### NORTH — Long-Term Context  
Does the decision make sense when foreseeable regulatory, land-use, environmental, or hazard-related context is considered?

### ALIGNED — Coherence  
Are the client’s stated objectives coherent with known constraints, risks, and structural realities?

If any element fails, proceeding may increase the risk of misunderstanding or harm.

---

## What You Receive

Clients receive a written **TESGI Advisory Decision Memo** that:
- summarizes objectives in the client’s own words,
- identifies known facts and uncertainties,
- applies the True / North / Aligned framework,
- and concludes with a clear decision state:
  - Proceed
  - Pause (with conditions)
  - Avoid / Walk Away

The memo is designed to support informed choice — not persuasion.

---

## Important Boundaries

- This service does not replace legal, engineering, surveying, or professional real estate advice.
- Analysis is based on publicly available information and reasonable inquiry.
- Clients retain full decision-making authority at all times.
- Declining to proceed is considered a valid and responsible outcome.

---

## Who This Is For

TESGI Advisory is suitable for clients who:
- feel uncertain or pressured,
- want clarity before committing,
- prefer realism over optimism,
- and value avoided mistakes as much as successful actions.

This service may not be suitable for clients seeking urgency, validation, or transactional advocacy.

---

TESGI Advisory helps people decide **whether a decision actually makes sense** — before it becomes irreversible.
