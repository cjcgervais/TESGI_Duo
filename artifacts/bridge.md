## 1. Purpose of This Bridge

This document formally bridges **TESGI Advisory (Core Advisory MVP)** into a **new project space** while preserving:

- governance intent,
- ethical and operational constraints,
- decision logic,
- and role boundaries.

It exists to ensure that the new project:
- does not re-litigate foundational decisions,
- does not accidentally assume representational authority,
- and does not dilute trust or scope through omission.

This bridge is authoritative for intent and constraints, not for implementation details.

## 4. Core Governance Posture (Non-Negotiable)

The following principles are **hard constraints** carried forward:

### 4.1 Role Boundary
- This work is **advisory**, not representational.
- No agency, brokerage, or fiduciary transaction role is created.
- No negotiation, coordination, or transaction execution is performed.

### 4.2 No Guarantees or Predictions
- No outcomes are promised.
- No approvals, values, timelines, or future states are predicted.
- Uncertainty is preserved where it exists.

### 4.3 Decision Authority
- Clients retain full decision-making authority.
- The advisor’s role is to clarify, not persuade.
- “Walk away” is a valid and successful outcome.

These constraints may not be softened without explicit documentation.

---

## 5. Canonical Decision Kernel (Carried Forward)

All advisory reasoning in the new project must pass the same kernel:

### TRUE
Are material facts accurate, verifiable, and clearly bounded?

### NORTH
Does the decision make sense over time, given known constraints?

### ALIGNED
Do objectives, risks, assumptions, and structure actually fit together?

Failure of any leg requires:
- pause, or
- avoidance / refusal,
not workaround or reframing.

---

## 6. Scope Discipline (What This Project Is Not)

The new project must **not** drift into:
- representation by implication,
- hybrid advisory + agent roles,
- outcome advocacy,
- ESG marketing or validation services,
- pressure-based framing.

If external parties request these, the correct response is:
- clarification,
- boundary reinforcement,
- or refusal.

---

## 7. Language Discipline

### Internal Language
- May be precise, analytical, and explicit.
- May refer to risk, harm, and refusal candidly.

### External / Client Language
- Must be calm, plain, and non-promotional.
- Must avoid certainty, urgency, or persuasive framing.
- Must clearly state limits.

Internal language must never leak externally without translation.

## 8. Relationship to Real Estate Licensing or Brokerage

This project:
- does **not** require real estate licensing,
- does **not** act as a substitute for brokerage,
- and does **not** imply future activation of a realty arm.

Any future interaction with licensed activity must be:

No assumptions of automatic escalation are permitted.

## 9. Change Control

Any change that:
- expands scope,
- weakens boundaries,
- introduces outcome language,
- or increases regulatory ambiguity

must be:
1. documented,
2. justified,
3. and consciously accepted.

Silent drift is prohibited.
