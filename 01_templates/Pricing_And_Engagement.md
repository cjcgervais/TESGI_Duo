# TESGI Advisory — Pricing & Engagement

## Service Format

TESGI Advisory provides a **one-time, non-representational decision analysis** resulting in a written Decision Memo.

The service is designed to help clients decide whether to:
- proceed,
- pause and gather more information,
- or avoid a decision entirely.

The outcome may include a recommendation to walk away.

---

## Scope of Work (Included)

- Review of client-provided context and objectives
- Desktop analysis using publicly available information
- Application of the True / North / Aligned decision framework
- Preparation of a written Advisory Decision Memo (PDF)
- One follow-up clarification email, if needed

---

## Explicit Exclusions

This service does not include:
- real estate representation or agency services
- negotiation or transaction support
- site visits or inspections
- coordination with agents, municipalities, or regulators
- guarantees, predictions, or approvals
- valuation, appraisal, or legal advice

---

## Fee

**Flat fee:** $650 CAD  
(Payable in advance)

This fee reflects time, judgment, and avoided risk — not transaction outcomes.

---

## Timing

- Typical turnaround: **5–7 business days** from receipt of intake and payment
- Timing may vary depending on information completeness

---

## Important Notes

- This service provides analysis, not directives.
- Clients retain full decision-making authority.
- A recommendation to pause or avoid is considered a valid outcome.
- TESGI reserves the right to decline an engagement if competent analysis is not possible.

---

## Engagement Confirmation

Proceeding with this service confirms understanding that:
- no representation relationship is created,
- no outcomes are guaranteed,
- and this advisory memo supports — but does not replace — other professional advice.
