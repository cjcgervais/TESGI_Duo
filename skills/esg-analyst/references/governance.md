# Governance Assessment Reference

## Purpose

This reference guides governance risk evaluation for land acquisition decisions. Governance failures can result in title disputes, compliance violations, or structural problems that undermine the entire transaction.

## Assessment Categories

### 1. Title and Ownership

#### Chain of Title
| Check | Data Source | Risk Indicator |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Title search | Land registry / titles office | Current registered owner |
| Historical title | Title search (30+ years) | Ownership chain breaks |
| Survey accuracy | Licensed surveyor | Boundary alignment |
| Plan of subdivision | Titles office | Lot boundaries |

**Red Flags:**
- Gap in title history
- Unregistered dealings
- Survey discrepancies with fences/occupation
- Adverse possession claims

#### Encumbrances
| Encumbrance Type | Impact | Data Source |
|------------------|--------|-------------|
| Mortgage / charge | Must be discharged at settlement | Title search |
| Caveat | May block transfer | Title search |
| Restrictive covenant | Use limitations | Title search, covenant document |
| Easement | Access/use by others | Title search, survey |
| Lease | Ongoing obligations | Title search, lease documents |
| Profit a prendre | Extraction rights | Title search |

**Critical Checks:**
- Read actual covenant/easement documents, not just title notations
- Confirm easement locations on survey
- Verify lease terms and tenant rights

#### Disputes and Claims
| Check | Data Source | Risk Indicator |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Boundary disputes | Seller disclosure, neighbor inquiry | Active disagreement |
| Adverse possession | Occupation inconsistent with title | Long-term encroachment |
| Family law claims | Seller disclosure | Property of relationship |
| Bankruptcy / insolvency | ASIC, PPSR | Seller financial distress |

### 2. Regulatory Compliance

#### Zoning and Planning
| Check | Data Source | Risk Indicator |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Zone classification | Planning certificate | Permitted uses |
| Overlay controls | Planning certificate | Additional requirements |
| Use rights | Planning history | Existing use rights |
| Non-conforming use | Planning advice | Grandfathered status |

**Key Questions:**
- Is intended use permitted as-of-right?
- If permit required, what is approval pathway?
- Are there call-in or appeal risks?
- What conditions are likely?

#### Building and Development
| Check | Data Source | Risk Indicator |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Building permits | Council records | Outstanding permits |
| Occupancy certificates | Council records | Final sign-off |
| Illegal structures | Building inspection | Unpermitted works |
| Pool compliance | Pool safety register | Certificate currency |
| Essential services | Council records | Fire safety compliance |

**Red Flags:**
- Structures without permits
- Expired occupancy certificates
- Pool without current compliance certificate
- Outstanding building orders

#### Environmental Compliance
| Check | Data Source | Risk Indicator |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| EPA notices | EPA public register | Contamination orders |
| Environmental audit | EPA overlay register | Audit conditions |
| Waste licenses | EPA | Operating licenses |
| Pollution incidents | EPA, media | Historical issues |

### 3. Structural and Commercial

#### Ownership Entity
| Check | Consideration | Risk Indicator |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| Entity type | Tax, liability, succession | Inappropriate structure |
| Trust deed review | If trust purchaser | Deed limitations |
| Company status | ASIC search | Deregistration risk |
| Foreign investment | FIRB | Approval required |
| State surcharges | State revenue office | Additional duty |

**Common Structural Issues:**
- Personal purchase vs company/trust without advice
- SMSF property rules not understood
- Foreign purchaser surcharges not budgeted
- Joint tenancy vs tenants in common implications

#### Financing
| Check | Data Source | Risk Indicator |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Finance approval | Lender | Conditional/unconditional |
| Valuation | Lender valuation | Shortfall risk |
| Serviceability | Lender assessment | Capacity constraints |
| Special conditions | Loan documents | Unusual requirements |

#### Exit Strategy
| Check | Consideration | Risk Indicator |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| Resale market | Market analysis | Liquidity |
| Subdivision potential | Planning advice | Future flexibility |
| Lease exit | Lease terms | Break costs |
| Development timeline | Project feasibility | Holding costs |

### 4. Conflicts and Disclosure

#### Seller Disclosure
| Check | Data Source | Risk Indicator |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Vendor statement | Section 32/Vendor disclosure | Completeness |
| Material defects | Seller disclosure, inspection | Undisclosed issues |
| Disputes/notices | Seller disclosure | Outstanding matters |
| Insurance claims | Seller inquiry | Claim history |

**Disclosure Red Flags:**
- Reluctance to answer questions
- Inconsistencies between documents and statements
- "As is" sale without explanation
- Short settlement pressure

#### Agent and Advisor Conflicts
| Check | Consideration | Risk Indicator |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| Dual agency | Agent disclosure | Agent acts for both parties |
| Related party | Seller disclosure | Seller/agent relationship |
| Commission arrangements | Agent disclosure | Incentive conflicts |
| Advisor independence | Advisor disclosure | Referral arrangements |

## Due Diligence Timeline

Governance checks often have long lead times:

| Check | Typical Timeframe |
|-------|-------------------|
| Title search | 1-2 days |
| Survey | 1-2 weeks |
| Planning certificate | 1-5 days |
| Building permit search | 1-2 weeks |
| FIRB approval | 30-90 days |
| Contamination audit | 4-8 weeks |

**Critical:** If due diligence period is shorter than required checks, this is itself a governance risk. Recommend extension or conditional approach.
