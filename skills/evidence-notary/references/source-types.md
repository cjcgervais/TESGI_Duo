# Source Types Reference

## Purpose

This reference provides detailed classification of source types commonly encountered in land acquisition advisory, with guidance on reliability assessment and appropriate use.

## Tier 1: Authoritative Primary Sources

These sources are produced by authoritative bodies and carry high evidentiary weight.

### Title and Ownership

| Source | Provider | Reliability | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| Certificate of Title | State land registry | Very High | Definitive ownership record |
| Title search | State land registry | Very High | Current encumbrances, caveats |
| Historical title | State land registry | High | Chain of ownership |
| Survey plan | Licensed surveyor (registered) | Very High | Boundary definition |

### Planning and Zoning

| Source | Provider | Reliability | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| Planning certificate (s149/s32) | Local council | Very High | Official zoning statement |
| Planning scheme | State/local government | Very High | Applicable controls |
| Development approval | Local council | Very High | Approved use/works |
| Building permit | Local council/certifier | Very High | Approved construction |

### Environmental and Heritage

| Source | Provider | Reliability | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| EPA register entry | Environmental Protection Authority | Very High | Contamination status |
| AHIMS search | State heritage authority | Very High | Aboriginal heritage sites |
| Native title search | National Native Title Tribunal | Very High | Native title claims |
| Flood mapping | State/local authority | High | Flood zone designation |

### Professional Assessments

| Source | Provider | Reliability | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|

| BAL assessment | Accredited assessor | High | Bushfire attack level |
| Phase 1 ESA | Environmental consultant | High | Contamination risk |

## Tier 2: Professional Secondary Sources

These sources are produced by qualified professionals but may involve judgment or estimation.

### Valuations and Market Data

| Source | Provider | Reliability | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| Sworn valuation | Certified valuer | Medium-High | Opinion of value |
| Bank valuation | Lender's valuer | Medium | May be conservative |
| Comparable sales | Real estate databases | Medium | Historical, may not match |
| Market report | Industry analysts | Medium | Aggregated data |

### Professional Opinions

| Source | Provider | Reliability | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| Legal advice | Solicitor | Medium-High | Opinion on legal matters |
| Planning advice | Town planner | Medium-High | Opinion on approval prospects |
| Building quote | Licensed builder | Medium | Estimate, subject to variation |
| Engineering opinion | Engineer | Medium-High | Professional assessment |

## Tier 3: Informal Sources

These sources should be verified before reliance.

### Vendor/Agent Information

| Source | Provider | Reliability | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| Agent marketing | Real estate agent | Low | Promotional, verify independently |
| Vendor disclosure | Property seller | Low-Medium | Self-interested, verify key claims |
| Vendor statement (s32) | Vendor's solicitor | Medium | Formal but vendor-provided |
| Agent verbal claims | Real estate agent | Low | Not binding, verify in writing |

### Third-Party Information

| Source | Provider | Reliability | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| Neighbor information | Adjacent owners | Low | May be inaccurate or biased |
| Online reviews | Public websites | Very Low | Unverified, potentially false |
| Social media | Various | Very Low | Not reliable |
| Historical anecdotes | Local knowledge | Low | May be inaccurate |

### Client-Provided Information

| Source | Provider | Reliability | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| Client's stated objectives | Client | Medium | May evolve, confirm understanding |
| Client's assumptions | Client | Low | Verify against evidence |
| Client's prior experience | Client | Low-Medium | May not apply to this property |

## Tier 4: Unverified / Missing

These represent gaps in evidence that must be disclosed.

### Types of Missing Evidence

| Gap Type | Description | Impact |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Assessment not conducted | Required test/report not done | Cannot verify claim |
| Document not obtained | Exists but not reviewed | Cannot confirm content |
| Information unavailable | Does not exist or cannot be obtained | Permanent uncertainty |
| Outdated information | Exists but may be stale | Reliability degraded |

### Common Missing Sources in Land Transactions

| Missing Source | Why Critical | Recommended Action |
|----------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Soil test | Buildability, foundation costs | Commission geotechnical report |
| BAL assessment | Bushfire risk, build cost | Commission BAL assessment |
| Contamination assessment | Environmental liability | Commission Phase 1 ESA |
| Survey | Boundary accuracy | Commission new survey |
| Title search | Current ownership/encumbrances | Order title search |

## Source Quality Factors

### Factors That Increase Reliability

- Produced by government or statutory authority
- Prepared by licensed/accredited professional
- Recent date (within appropriate timeframe)
- First-hand observation or measurement
- Subject to professional liability
- Verifiable against independent sources

### Factors That Decrease Reliability

- Self-interested party (vendor, agent)
- Informal or verbal only
- Outdated (stale information)
- Second-hand or hearsay
- Unverifiable claims
- Promotional or marketing purpose
- Anonymous or unattributed

## Verification Checklist

For each key source, verify:

- [ ] Source authenticity (is it genuine?)
- [ ] Source authority (is provider qualified?)
- [ ] Source currency (is it current enough?)
- [ ] Source relevance (does it address the claim?)
- [ ] Source completeness (does it cover the full scope?)
- [ ] Source independence (any conflicts of interest?)
