# NORTH Assessment Reference

## Core Question

**Does the decision make sense over time given known constraints?**

## Assessment Criteria

### 1. Regulatory Context

| Check | Pass Condition | Fail Condition |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Zoning compliance | Current use/plan aligns with zoning | Zoning conflict or variance required |
| Permit pathway | Clear path to required approvals | Uncertain or blocked approval route |
| Regulatory trajectory | Stable or favorable regulatory direction | Adverse regulatory changes anticipated |
| Compliance history | No material violations | Outstanding violations or enforcement |

### 2. Environmental & Hazard Exposure

| Check | Pass Condition | Fail Condition |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Flood risk | Outside high-risk zones or mitigated | Unmitigated flood exposure |
| Environmental constraints | No critical habitat/contamination issues | Environmental barriers present |
| Natural hazard exposure | Acceptable risk profile | Elevated hazard exposure |
| Climate considerations | Resilient to foreseeable climate impacts | Vulnerable to climate-related risks |

### 3. Infrastructure & Servicing

| Check | Pass Condition | Fail Condition |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Access | Legal and physical access confirmed | Access uncertain or disputed |
| Utilities | Services available or obtainable | Servicing gaps or prohibitive costs |
| Capacity | Infrastructure can support intended use | Capacity constraints identified |

### 4. Timeline Viability

| Check | Pass Condition | Fail Condition |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Approval timelines | Realistic given regulatory processes | Unrealistic timeline assumptions |
| Market timing | Not dependent on specific market conditions | High market-timing dependency |
| Constraint evolution | Constraints stable over decision horizon | Constraints likely to worsen |

## Long-Term Context Factors

Consider foreseeable changes over the relevant time horizon:

- **5-year horizon**: Regulatory cycles, market cycles, infrastructure plans
- **10-year horizon**: Climate adaptation, demographic shifts, policy evolution
- **20+ year horizon**: Structural changes, generational factors

## Failure Modes

The NORTH leg fails when:

1. Regulatory barriers exist with no clear resolution path
2. Environmental or hazard exposure creates material long-term risk
3. Infrastructure constraints cannot be reasonably resolved
4. Timeline assumptions are unrealistic given known processes
5. Decision depends on conditions likely to change adversely

## Governance Note

Per bridge.md: "No outcomes are promised. No approvals, values, timelines, or future states are predicted." The NORTH assessment identifies context and constraints — it does not forecast outcomes.
