# ALIGNED Assessment Reference

## Core Question

**Do objectives, assumptions, and structure actually fit together?**

## Assessment Criteria

### 1. Objective-Constraint Mapping

| Check | Pass Condition | Fail Condition |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Goal clarity | Client objectives clearly stated | Vague or conflicting objectives |
| Constraint awareness | Client understands material constraints | Constraints ignored or minimized |
| Goal-constraint fit | Objectives achievable within constraints | Fundamental conflict exists |
| Priority coherence | Priorities logically ordered | Contradictory priority claims |

### 2. Assumption Validity

| Check | Pass Condition | Fail Condition |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Explicit assumptions | Key assumptions documented | Hidden or unstated assumptions |
| Assumption testing | Assumptions validated against facts | Assumptions untested or unfounded |
| Dependency mapping | Assumption dependencies understood | Cascading assumption risks |
| Sensitivity analysis | Outcome robust to assumption variation | Outcome fragile to assumption changes |

### 3. Structural Coherence

| Check | Pass Condition | Fail Condition |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Decision structure | Clear decision pathway | Confused or circular logic |
| Resource alignment | Resources match requirements | Resource-requirement mismatch |
| Timeline-action fit | Actions achievable in timeline | Timeline-action disconnect |
| Risk-reward coherence | Risk profile matches stated tolerance | Risk profile exceeds stated tolerance |

### 4. Risk-Goal Fit Analysis

| Check | Pass Condition | Fail Condition |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Risk identification | Material risks documented | Risks overlooked or minimized |
| Risk tolerance match | Risks align with client's stated tolerance | Risks exceed stated tolerance |
| Mitigation viability | Risk mitigations realistic | Mitigations depend on hope or luck |
| Downside clarity | Client understands downside scenarios | Downside scenarios obscured |

## Coherence Test Questions

Apply these diagnostic questions:

1. **Can the client articulate what success looks like?**
2. **Are the constraints they've accepted actually constraints they control?**
3. **Do their assumptions survive contact with verified facts?**
4. **Does the structure of their plan account for the risks they've acknowledged?**
5. **Would a neutral observer see alignment, or rationalization?**

## Failure Modes

The ALIGNED leg fails when:

1. Client objectives fundamentally conflict with known constraints
2. Critical assumptions are unfounded or untestable
3. Decision structure contains logical inconsistencies
4. Risk profile exceeds client's stated or demonstrated tolerance
5. Coherence exists only through selective attention or wishful thinking

## Red Flags

- "It will work out" without specific mechanism
- Dismissing constraints as "manageable" without evidence
- Changing objectives when constraints emerge
- Assuming best-case scenarios as base case
- Relying on others to solve problems outside client's control

## Governance Note

Per bridge.md: "The advisor's role is to clarify, not persuade." The ALIGNED assessment surfaces misalignment honestly — it does not rationalize or reframe to achieve coherence.
