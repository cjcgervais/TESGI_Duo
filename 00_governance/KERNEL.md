This file is normative for TESGI Advisory OS. Invalid states are unbuildable.

## TRUE
- Claims trace to artifacts or explicit assumptions.
- Unknowns are labeled and scoped.
- Evidence quality is stated.

## NORTH
- Advisory impact is prioritized over volume.
- Decisions optimize for client clarity and accountability.
- Work remains bounded to advisory scope.

## ALIGNED
- Boundaries and language rules are enforced.
- Changes follow the defined control tiers.
- Packaging preserves integrity and traceability.
