# NORTH Assessment

**Case:** CASE_0006 - Foster Unpermitted Granny Flat
**Date:** 2024-07-05

## Long-Term Context Evaluation

### Regulatory Context

| Factor | Current State | Outlook | Risk Level |
|--------|---------------|---------|------------|
| Building compliance | Non-compliant (unpermitted) | Enforcement risk ongoing | HIGH |
| Electrical safety | Substandard in granny flat | Must be rectified | HIGH |
| Rental legality | Unlawful without occupancy cert | Cannot legally rent | HIGH |
| Title covenant | Height restriction | Permanent | Low |

### Compliance Pathway Options

| Option | Process | Cost Estimate | Timeline | Success Probability |
|--------|---------|---------------|----------|---------------------|
| Retrospective permit | Application + upgrades | $15,000-$40,000 | 3-6 months | Medium (if meets standards) |
| Demolish granny flat | Remove structure | $10,000-$15,000 | 2-4 weeks | Certain (loses rental income) |
| Do nothing | Accept enforcement risk | $0 upfront | N/A | Ongoing liability |

### Financial Impact Analysis

| Scenario | Impact on Value Proposition |
|----------|----------------------------|
| Rental income as assumed ($350/wk) | Only achievable if retrospective permit granted |
| Retrospective permit cost | $15,000-$40,000 additional investment |
| No permit / no rental | Property is just a house, no income offset |
| Enforcement action | Demolition cost + loss of structure value |

### Long-Term Ownership Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Council enforcement | Medium | High (demolition order) | Seek permit or disclose risk |
| Insurance rejection | High | High (uninsured loss) | Rectify electrical, confirm cover |
| Future sale difficulty | High | Medium (disclosure required) | Resolve before sale |
| Tenant injury claim | Medium | Very High (uninsured liability) | Do not rent until compliant |

### Observations

- 10+ year ownership assumption requires resolution of compliance issues
- Continuing rental without occupancy certificate creates liability exposure
- Electrical issues are safety hazards that must be addressed regardless
- Vendor's assurance ("no problems") transfers problem, not solution

### Risk Notes

- First home buyers have limited capacity to absorb unexpected costs
- $30,000 improvement budget may be consumed by compliance rectification
- Rental income strategy depends on resolving unpermitted status
- Low-moderate risk tolerance is inconsistent with accepting compliance gaps

## NORTH Status: UNCERTAIN

Decision viability depends on resolution of compliance issues. Without rectification plan and cost certainty, long-term ownership risks are unbounded.
