### Observations

**Title Encumbrances:**
- Restrictive covenant limits building height to 6 meters
- Covenant was registered in 1985, benefits neighboring properties
- Granny flat appears to comply with height limit
- Covenant cannot be removed without beneficiary consent

**Unpermitted Structures:**
- Granny flat has no building permit (required)
- No occupancy certificate (required for habitation)
- Vendor states "been there 20 years" - does not create legality
- Council could issue order to cease use or demolish
- Current rental income is from unlawful use

**Building Compliance Issues:**
- Main house: unpermitted bathroom renovation
- Granny flat: substandard electrical wiring
- Both require rectification for compliance

### Uncertainties

- Whether council will enforce against granny flat
- Cost to retrospectively permit granny flat (if possible)
- Whether granny flat can meet current building standards
- Whether insurer will cover unpermitted structure
- Impact on valuation and finance approval
