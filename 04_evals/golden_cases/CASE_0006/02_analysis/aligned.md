### Observations

- Core value proposition (rental income) depends on resolving compliance
- First home buyers' budget has no contingency for compliance costs
- Risk tolerance (low-moderate) is inconsistent with buying unpermitted structures
- Vendor is transferring compliance problem to buyer

### Risk Notes

- "20 years no problems" is vendor rhetoric, not legal protection
- Clients should not assume they can continue unlawful rental
- Electrical issues create personal safety and liability exposure
- Decision requires clarity on retrospective permit pathway and costs

## ALIGNED Status: CONDITIONAL

Objectives can only align if: (1) retrospective permit is achievable, (2) costs fit budget, (3) clients accept higher risk than stated tolerance. Without these conditions confirmed, objectives are misaligned.
