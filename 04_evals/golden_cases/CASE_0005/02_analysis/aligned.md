### Observations

- Client sees "reduced price" as opportunity; reality is "insufficient discount for liability"
- High risk tolerance does not substitute for adequate capital
- This opportunity requires: (1) remediation expertise, (2) $4M+ budget, (3) 8-10 year horizon
- Client has: (1) no stated remediation experience, (2) $1.5M budget, (3) 5-7 year horizon
- Every structural element is misaligned

### Risk Notes

- "Sophisticated investor" self-assessment may be overconfident for environmental deals
- Vendor's price reduction suggests they understand liability better than buyer
- Walking away protects client from capital loss
- There may be an opportunity here — but not for this client

## ALIGNED Status: FAIL

Fundamental misalignment between client resources/timeline and project requirements. Budget shortfall of $2.3M makes this transaction economically irrational for this buyer.
