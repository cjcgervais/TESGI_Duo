### Observations

- Contamination is definitively confirmed, not speculative
- Contaminants: petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH, BTEX) in soil and groundwater
- Concentrations exceed both residential AND commercial/industrial thresholds
- Site is on EPA Contaminated Land Register (public record)
- EPA notice registered on title (travels with land)
- Preliminary remediation estimate: $1.8M - $3.2M
- Price reduction ($1.4M → $890K) reflects contamination, but may be insufficient

### Uncertainties

- Final remediation cost (range is wide: $1.8M - $3.2M)
- Remediation timeline (typically 2-5 years for groundwater)
- Whether EPA will approve site for residential use post-remediation
- Long-term monitoring requirements and costs
- Potential for off-site migration requiring expanded remediation

### Evidence Quality

**Rating:** High

Contamination is confirmed through professional assessment. Uncertainty relates to remediation scope, not contamination existence.

### Risk Notes

- Groundwater contamination typically requires long-term treatment
- BTEX (benzene) contamination triggers highest regulatory scrutiny
- EPA approval for residential use is not guaranteed even after remediation
- Off-site migration could expand liability to adjacent properties
- Insurance for contamination risk is expensive or unavailable

## TRUE Status: PASS (facts are clearly bounded, but severely adverse)

Material facts are verifiable and available. The facts indicate severe adverse conditions.
