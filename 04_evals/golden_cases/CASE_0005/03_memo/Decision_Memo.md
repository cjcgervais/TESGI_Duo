## 7. Rationale Summary

This is not a value opportunity for you. It is a capital trap. The reasons are:

1. **Remediation costs exceed your entire remaining budget.** After purchasing for $890K, you would have $610K remaining. Remediation will cost $1.8M-$3.2M. You cannot fund the remediation.

2. **The discount is insufficient.** The vendor reduced the price by $510K. The contamination creates $1.8M-$3.2M in liability. You are being asked to accept $1.3M-$2.7M in unfunded liability.

3. **Your timeline is consumed by remediation.** Groundwater contamination requires 2-5 years of active treatment plus monitoring. Your 5-7 year hold period ends before development can begin.

4. **Benzene contamination triggers highest regulatory scrutiny.** BTEX contamination (including benzene, a known carcinogen) means EPA will require extensive remediation and long-term monitoring before approving any sensitive use.

5. **This opportunity requires a different buyer.** A buyer with $5M+ budget, environmental remediation expertise, and 10-year timeline might find value here. You do not match that profile.

### The Price Reduction Is Not An Opportunity

When a seller reduces price by $510K on a property with $2M+ in contamination liability, they are not offering you a deal. They are attempting to transfer liability to you. The "discount" still leaves you holding unfunded obligations.
