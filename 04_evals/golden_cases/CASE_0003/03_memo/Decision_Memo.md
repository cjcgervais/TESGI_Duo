## 1. Client Objective (As Understood)

You are considering purchasing a 2.5-acre former automotive repair workshop at 15 Industrial Lane, Westbrook for $285,000. Your intention is to redevelop the site for residential townhouses (6-8 units) over a 3-5 year timeline.

Your stated priorities are:
- Redevelopment for residential use
- Medium-term investment horizon
- Maximize return on capital

Your risk tolerance is moderate-high as an experienced developer, but you want to understand environmental exposure.

## 2. Information Snapshot

### Known Facts
- Property is 2.5 acres, zoned Mixed Use (MUZ)
- Residential development is permitted under zoning
- Title is clear
- Former use was automotive repair workshop
- Vendor has disclosed "oil staining on concrete slab"
- 5 days remain in 14-day due diligence period

### Key Uncertainties / Gaps
- No Phase 1 Environmental Site Assessment completed
- No Phase 2 ESA (soil/groundwater sampling) completed
- No EPA records search completed
- Contamination type, extent, and depth unknown
- Remediation requirements and costs unknown
- Whether EPA audit condition will apply unknown

**These gaps are material and prevent informed decision-making.**

## 3. TRUE Assessment Summary

Critical environmental information is not available. The property's former use as an automotive workshop creates high probability of contamination. Vendor disclosure of oil staining confirms at minimum surface contamination exists. Without Phase 1 and Phase 2 ESA, the extent and remediation cost cannot be determined.

**TRUE Status: FAIL — Unbounded uncertainty on material facts**

## 7. Missing Information List

Before a decision can be made, the following must be obtained:

1. **Phase 1 Environmental Site Assessment**
   - Desktop review of historical use
   - EPA records search
   - Site inspection by environmental consultant
   - Estimated time: 2-3 weeks
   - Estimated cost: $3,000-$5,000

2. **Phase 2 Environmental Site Assessment** (if Phase 1 indicates risk)
   - Soil and groundwater sampling
   - Laboratory analysis
   - Contamination extent mapping
   - Estimated time: 4-6 weeks
   - Estimated cost: $10,000-$25,000

3. **Remediation Cost Estimate** (if contamination confirmed)
   - Remediation Action Plan
   - Cost estimate from licensed remediation contractor
   - Timeline estimate

4. **EPA Pre-Application Advice** (recommended)
   - Confirm whether audit condition will apply
   - Understand pathway to residential classification

---

## 8. Recommended Path Forward

1. **Request due diligence extension** — Minimum 6 weeks to complete Phase 1 and Phase 2 ESA
2. **Commission Phase 1 ESA immediately** — If vendor declines extension, consider making offer conditional on satisfactory environmental assessment
3. **Do not proceed unconditionally** — The current information gap creates unbounded liability risk
4. **Consider walking away** if vendor refuses reasonable due diligence timeline
