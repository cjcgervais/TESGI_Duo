## 1. Client Objective (As Understood)

You are considering purchasing 85 acres of rural land at Murrundi Creek Road, Yarran for $480,000. Your stated objectives are:

1. Build a weekend retreat / holiday home
2. Potential future subdivision (2-3 lots)
3. Passive income from grazing lease
4. Long-term family asset (generational wealth)

Your risk tolerance is moderate, with emphasis on a clear pathway to development.

---

## 2. Information Snapshot

### Known Facts
- Property is 85 acres, zoned RU1 Primary Production
- Freehold title, no caveats
- Dwelling is permitted under zoning (subject to approvals)
- AHIMS search identifies **3 registered Aboriginal heritage sites** on the property
- Site types include: scarred tree, artifact scatter, **potential burial site**
- Active **native title claim** (Yarran Peoples) covers this area
- No Indigenous Land Use Agreement exists

### Key Constraints
- Heritage sites protected under NSW National Parks and Wildlife Act 1974
- Disturbing Aboriginal objects: penalties up to $1.1 million and/or imprisonment
- Any ground disturbance requires Aboriginal Heritage Impact Permit (AHIP)
- AHIP process: 12-24 months, outcome uncertain
- Native title determination: typically 5-10 years

Heritage constraints are definitively established through AHIMS and NNTT searches. Three registered sites and an active native title claim create clear, verifiable barriers to development.

**TRUE Status: PASS (constraints are known and bounded)**

## 7. Rationale Summary

This property is not suitable for your stated objectives. The key reasons are:

1. **Heritage sites prevent practical development.** Three registered Aboriginal heritage sites, including a potential burial site, create severe constraints on any ground disturbance. The AHIP process takes 12-24 months with uncertain outcome, and approval is less likely given the presence of a potential burial site.

2. **Native title claim adds further uncertainty.** The active native title claim will take 5-10 years to determine. Development and subdivision may require negotiation with or consent from native title holders, whose rights are not yet defined.

3. **Your timeline is incompatible with reality.** A 90-day settlement assumes you can proceed with development planning. In practice, you would be purchasing land where development rights are uncertain for 5+ years.

4. **Your objectives require development that may never be approved.** Building a dwelling and subdividing are your primary goals. This property cannot deliver those goals within any reasonable timeframe.

5. **The price reflects the constraints.** At ~$5,600/acre, this land is priced for grazing with limited development potential. Purchasing at this price for development would be overpaying for rights you may never obtain.

### Walking Away Is the Correct Outcome

This is not a failure of due diligence or negotiation. This property is simply not suitable for your objectives. There will be other properties. This one is not aligned with what you are trying to achieve.
