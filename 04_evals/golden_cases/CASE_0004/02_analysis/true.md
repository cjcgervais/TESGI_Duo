### Observations

- AHIMS search confirms 3 Aboriginal heritage sites registered on the property
- Site types include: scarred tree, artifact scatter, potential burial site
- Heritage sites are protected under NSW National Parks and Wildlife Act 1974
- Harming Aboriginal objects carries penalties up to $1.1 million and/or imprisonment
- Active native title claim (Yarran Peoples) covers the property area
- Native title determination process typically takes 5-10 years
- No Indigenous Land Use Agreement (ILUA) exists for this area

### Uncertainties

- Exact location of heritage sites relative to potential building envelope
- Whether Aboriginal Heritage Impact Permit (AHIP) would be granted
- Timeline for native title determination
- Whether native title holders would consent to development
- Full extent of heritage significance (only registered sites known)

### Evidence Quality

**Rating:** Moderate-High for constraints; Low for resolution pathway

Heritage constraints are definitively established. Pathway to development is highly uncertain.

### Risk Notes

- Heritage sites cannot be lawfully disturbed without AHIP
- AHIP process can take 12-24 months with no guarantee of approval
- Potential burial site creates highest level of cultural sensitivity
- Native title claim creates additional layer of uncertainty
- Development may require consent from traditional owners (not yet determined)

## TRUE Status: PASS (with severe constraints)

Facts are verifiable and clearly bounded. The constraints are known, but they indicate fundamental barriers.
