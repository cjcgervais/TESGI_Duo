### Observations

- Client objectives require development rights that do not practically exist
- Heritage constraints are not "problems to solve" but permanent features
- Purchasing for development would be purchasing based on false assumptions
- Property may suit a buyer seeking undeveloped rural land with cultural significance
- This buyer's objectives do not match this property's characteristics

### Risk Notes

- Clients appear unaware of the severity of heritage constraints
- "Can we build?" answer is technically "maybe in 5+ years with uncertain outcome"
- Practical answer is "this property is not suitable for your objectives"
- Avoiding this purchase is not a failure; it is appropriate filtering
