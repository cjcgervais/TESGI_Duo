- Subdivision objective is likely incompatible with heritage constraints
- Passive grazing use may be only viable near-term use
- Property value reflects current constraints (relatively low $/acre)

### Risk Notes

- Client's 90-day settlement assumes constraints can be managed - this is not realistic
- Long-term hold without development may not meet client's objectives

- Heritage protection is constitutionally embedded and unlikely to weaken
